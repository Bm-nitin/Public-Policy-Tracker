Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Policy Tracker may collect, use, process, and protect information when you access or use the platform. Policy Tracker is an academic and educational project.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, the platform may process the following information:

  • Name and email address provided when creating an account.
  • A securely stored password hash rather than your original password.
  • Authentication and session information required to keep you signed in.
  • Email-verification and password-reset token information. Security tokens are stored in protected form and are designed to expire and become unusable after their intended use.
  • Information contained in requests that you voluntarily submit through the platform, such as policy-related questions.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to create and authenticate your account, verify your email address, provide password-reset functionality, maintain secure sessions, respond to your requests, operate the platform, prevent misuse, and improve the reliability and security of the project.

3. AI Processing

Some policy-related requests may be processed by an external artificial intelligence service to generate or assist with a response. Information included in a request may therefore be transmitted to that service when AI processing is used.

Do not submit passwords, payment-card information, government identification numbers, confidential credentials, or other sensitive personal information in chatbot queries. AI providers may have their own terms, retention practices, and privacy policies.

4. Authentication and Cookies

The platform uses a session cookie to maintain authenticated sessions. The authentication cookie is designed to use security attributes such as HttpOnly and Secure in production, together with an appropriate SameSite setting for the platform's frontend and backend architecture.

This cookie is used for authentication and security rather than advertising or behavioral tracking. Other cookies or tracking technologies may be introduced if the platform's structure changes, in which case this policy will be updated as appropriate.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the platform, such as hosting, infrastructure, email delivery, database, or artificial intelligence providers. Such providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect account and authentication information. Passwords are not stored as plain text. Authentication and reset mechanisms are designed to use securely generated tokens, expiration, and server-side controls.

No internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should use a strong, unique password and avoid submitting information that you do not need to provide.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only as necessary for the purposes for which it is processed, for security and operational requirements, or as otherwise required by applicable law. Exact retention periods may vary by data type and service.

8. Your Choices and Requests

You should contact the project through the available website contact method if you have a question about your information, account, or this Privacy Policy. Requests may be subject to reasonable verification before action is taken.

9. Children's Privacy

The platform is intended for general educational use. Users should not provide personal information belonging to another person, including a child, without appropriate authorization.

10. External Links and Services

The platform may refer users to official government websites or other external services. Once you leave Policy Tracker, the privacy practices of the external website or service apply.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the platform's architecture, features, data processing, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

12. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please use the contact method provided on the Policy Tracker website.
